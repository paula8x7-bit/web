English speaking nutritionist in Madrid

Consultations from home, by video call, and your blood test at a centre in Madrid.

I'm Paula Rubio, a registered dietitian-nutritionist with a clinical and integrative approach, and a long haul Inflight Manager. I consult online in English and Spanish, so it doesn't matter that you live in Madrid: we work by video call or with a plan in the app, and if you need a blood test, you can have it done near home.

I work with personalized diet therapy for body composition, digestive health, autoimmunity, hormonal imbalances, fatigue and other cases that call for looking beyond an isolated meal plan.

Your blood test in Madrid

If you live in Madrid, you can get your blood tests done with Diagnoseum, the clinical testing project I co-founded: you choose exactly what you want to test, no doctor's referral needed, backed by Eurofins.

You buy online what you want to test and have your blood drawn at any Eurofins or Abolab centre in Madrid. Then we go through the results together and I use them for your plan.
